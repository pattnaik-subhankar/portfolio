The Method

The Reframe Loop

Five steps, one loop. The same circuit CBT therapists train, compressed into a daily 10-minute journaling practice.

1

Catch

Pause at the emotional spike. You have a 6-second window before the amygdala drives the response.

2

Name

Label the specific emotion, not the vague one. Precision reduces reactivity.

3

Question

Name the distortion type. Attack it with one piece of counter-evidence.

4

Rewrite

Write the balanced thought in one sentence. True, not positive.

5

Log

Rate before and after, 1-10. The delta is your skill score. Watch it grow.

The Patterns

The 10 distortion types

Every automatic negative thought fits a pattern. Name it before you fight it. Catching the pattern is 50% of the reframe.

01
Catastrophizing

Assuming the worst case is the most likely case.

02
Black-and-White Thinking

Perfect or failure. No middle ground.

03
Mind-Reading

Assuming you know what others think, without evidence.

04
Overgeneralization

One bad event becomes a universal pattern.

05
Fortune-Telling

Predicting negative outcomes as if they were facts.

06
Emotional Reasoning

Treating feelings as facts. I feel it, therefore it is.

07
Should Statements

Rigid rules about how you and others must behave.

08
Labeling

One event becomes a global identity. I am a loser.

09
Personalization

Blaming yourself for what was outside your control.

10
Discounting the Positive

Minimizing achievements and compliments.
