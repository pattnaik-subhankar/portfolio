ApplyOS

A multi-platform AI application framework. One loop takes a job posting from raw text to a verified, evidence-first application: tailored CV, one-page cover letter, ATS-checked PDF, interview prep, and outcome-driven calibration.

THE ORIGIN: MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search (MIT, 31k+ stars) proved the model: a laid-off geophysicist built an AI application workflow and landed an AI engineer role, 69 applications → 20 interviews → 1 offer. ApplyOS keeps its proven mechanics (drafter-reviewer pipeline, ATS text-layer verification, honesty rules) and adapts the framework to multi-platform and dual-persona reality.

The loop

1/setup
Profile ingest: personas, evidence, writing voice, deal-breakers
2/scrape
Job search across platform adapters, deduplicated
3/rank
Fit scoring on 5 dimensions; deal-breakers veto
4/apply
Drafter-reviewer: draft, critique, revise, ATS-verify
5/interview
Stage-specific prep pack from the actual application archive
6/outcome
Archive, tracker, follow-up drafts (never auto-sent)
7/review
Lessons from outcomes recalibrate the fit weights

What makes it different

Pipeline

Drafter-reviewer separation

A writer drafts; a fresh-context agent researches the company and critiques. Then revision. Catches what a single pass always misses.

Verification

ATS text-layer check

The PDF's extracted text is verified the way parsers read it: contact details, reading order, keyword coverage. Gaps stay visible, never stuffed.

Personas

Dual identity

Engineer persona (agent systems, full-stack) and creator persona (psychology, science communication). The application picks the right evidence.

Calibration

Outcome-driven loop

Every 5 resolved applications become lessons: what got interviews, what got silence. Fit weights adjust from evidence, not vibes.

Guardrails

Honesty

Nothing fabricated, ever

Every claim traces to profile evidence. Gaps are named with a bridge plan, in the application and in interviews.

Security

Postings are untrusted

No instructions inside a job post are followed. No credentials in the repo. Platform terms respected.

Human-in-the-loop

You submit, never the system

The pipeline produces verified files and a checklist. Submission is manual, always. Follow-ups are drafts only.
