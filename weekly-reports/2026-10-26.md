One correction to how the counter reads. The Oct 25 run is counted as the fifth error, but that run did its work. A job that does the work and then fails to hand it over is worse than one that fails loudly, because the second one gets fixed.

Net: audit tooling was attempted, it currently would cry wolf, and it has no delivery path. Not a gain.
