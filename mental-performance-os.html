The Evidence

Built on 12 peer-reviewed concepts

Every protocol references primary research. Four of the twelve studies behind the curriculum.

Draganski et al. (2004)
Nature 427, 311-312

Juggling practice increased grey matter in the mid-temporal area. After 3 months without practice, it decreased. Direct evidence of experience-dependent neuroplasticity in adults.

PMID 14737157
Beck (1976)
Cognitive Therapy and the Emotional Disorders

Established the cognitive model: distorted thinking patterns cause and maintain depression and anxiety. The foundation of CBT, which shows roughly 75% efficacy.

FOUNDATION OF CBT
Schultz et al. (1997)
Science 275(5306), 1593-1599

Dopamine neurons fire during unexpected reward cues, not during reward delivery. Dopamine encodes motivational salience and prediction error, not pleasure.

PMID 9054347
Brewer et al. (2011)
PNAS 108(50), 20254-20259

Experienced meditators showed reduced default mode network activation. Ten to twenty minutes of daily meditation physically changes brain network architecture.
