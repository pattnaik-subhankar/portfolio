Built on 12 Peer-Reviewed Neuroscience Concepts

Every protocol in the 30-day challenge references primary research. Here are 4 of the 12 studies that power the curriculum.

Draganski et al. (2004)
Nature 427, 311-312
Juggling practice increased grey matter in the mid-temporal area. After 3 months without practice, grey matter decreased. Direct evidence of experience-dependent neuroplasticity in adults.
PMID 14737157
Beck (1976)
Cognitive Therapy and the Emotional Disorders
Established the cognitive model showing that distorted thinking patterns cause and maintain depression and anxiety. The foundation of Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, which has 75% efficacy.
—
Schultz et al. (1997)
Science 275(5306), 1593-1599
Dopamine neurons fire during unexpected reward cues, not during reward delivery. Dopamine encodes motivational salience and prediction error, not pleasure.
PMID 9054347
Brewer et al. (2011)
PNAS 108(50), 20254-20259
Experienced meditators showed reduced default mode network activation and altered DMN connectivity. 10-20 minutes of daily meditation physically changes brain network architecture.
PMID 22114193
