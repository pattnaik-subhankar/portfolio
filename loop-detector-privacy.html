Loop Detector · Privacy Policy

Chrome extension · Effective 2026-08-14

The short version: everything runs on your device. Loop Detector never uploads, transmits, or stores your conversations anywhere. There is no server, no account, no telemetry, and no analytics.

What the extension reads

  • When you open the side panel on a supported chat site (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok), the extension reads the visible conversation text in that tab to score it against the SIM-VAIL rubric (Weilnhammer et al., Nature Medicine 2026).
  • In paste mode, it reads only the text you paste into the panel.
  • The extension does not read pages on other sites and does not read your browsing history.

What happens to the data

  • The conversation text is processed in your browser's memory by the local scoring engine.
  • Nothing is written to disk except a risk badge count and panel UI state via chrome.storage (local only, never synced).
  • No network requests are made for analysis. The only outbound link is the optional "Open Loop Detector" button, which opens the web app in a new tab when you click it.

Crisis handling

If the on-device analysis detects self-harm or harm-to-others content, the panel shows crisis helpline numbers (Tele-MANAS 14416, Kiran 1800-599-0019, iCall). This is displayed locally; no alert is sent anywhere.

Data retention

None. Close the panel and the analysis is gone.
